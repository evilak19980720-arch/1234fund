In the three-level marketing model, each level of your members will contribute task income to you:
Your first Level Marketing members will give you 5% of his income from all tasks.
Your second Level Marketing members will give you 2% of his income from all tasks.
Your third Level Marketing members will give you 1% of his income from all tasks.

For example:
You invite 10 members, your first level members have 10 people, second level members have 100 people, third level members have 1000 people, each person’s daliy income is 1000 INR, you can imagine your daily earnings:
First Level Members give you：1000*10*5%=500 ₹
Second Level Members give you:1000*100*2%=2000 ₹
Third Level Members give you:1000*1000*1%=10000 ₹
All of your daily Three-level marketing task income is:500+2000+10000=12500 ₹

Tips: In the three-level marketing model, each level of your members must purchase a VIP to contribute task income to you,If your subordinates are not VIPs, you cannot enjoy this income.
